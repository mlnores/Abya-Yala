# Stylistic revision report

## 1. Removed repetition

- Consolidated the rationale for separating the internal catalogue, OpenAlex and Research Horizon evidence. The Introduction now explains the principle, Methods states how the sources are connected, Results reports their outputs and Discussion interprets their differences.
- Reduced repeated descriptions of the algorithmic and curated states. Methods retains the audit procedure, Results reports its measured effect and Discussion explains why the hybrid machine/expert process matters.
- Reduced repeated uses of "bounded," "construct-specific," "provenance," "inspectable," "semantic representation," and "evidence layers" where the limitation or distinction had already been established.
- Tightened the Conclusion so that the architecture and separation of the three sources are summarized once rather than restated in the closing paragraph.

The Introduction was reduced from 1,021 to 938 text words (8.1%), and the Discussion from 1,877 to 1,735 text words (7.6%), as measured with `texcount`.

## 2. Simplified sentences

- Split the Introduction's architecture sentence into a sequence covering internal topic construction, expert curation, longitudinal organization and links to external infrastructures.
- Recast the four-contribution paragraph into shorter statements while preserving all four contributions and their qualifications.
- Simplified the Methods descriptions of the study design, topic labels, line-level profile dimensions, Research Horizon screening and reproducibility conditions.
- Shortened the Results transitions and cross-source comparison without changing any reported values.
- Divided dense Discussion sentences on causal interpretation, OpenAlex scope, hybrid curation and model granularity into direct subject--verb statements.

## 3. Improved English

- Replaced "documentary retention of screened research-front candidates" with context-appropriate forms such as "retention after documentary review."
- Replaced "recorded candidate funnel" with "documented selection process."
- Replaced "publisher-level thematic system" with "the publisher's thematic structure" where the formal systems phrasing was unnecessary.
- Replaced "the analytical identity of each layer" with direct wording about not conflating the meanings of the sources.
- Replaced awkward formulations such as "historically accumulated lines" and "strong survival within the screened frontier set" with "lines with high historical volume" and "full retention among screened candidates."
- Reworded the cross-source traceability claim so that "the basis of cross-source interpretations can be inspected directly."

## 4. Deliberately preserved wording

- Retained "semantic units" where topics are formally introduced as the units of the representation.
- Retained "provenance" where it refers specifically to documentary or interpretive provenance rather than a general audit record.
- Retained the distinction between the initial algorithmic partition and curated state in Methods, Results and Discussion because each section serves a different purpose.
- Retained "bounded" where it remains technically useful for defined external comparisons, rather than as a repeated general qualifier.
- Retained OpenAlex as an "open scholarly graph" and preserved the explicit statement that the study performs no graph reasoning.
- Retained all caution concerning HDBSCAN noise, SPECTER2 transfer, translation, one-expert classification, retrospective thresholds, incomplete years, country selection, licensed Research Horizon data, cross-publisher validation and the absence of direct publisher-impact evidence.
